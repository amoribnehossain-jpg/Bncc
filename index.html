About BMARPC Air Wing

Part of Bangladesh National Cadet Corps (BNCC) – Second Line of Defense

Excellence in Leadership, Aviation & Service

The Bangladesh National Cadet Corps (BNCC) Air Wing at Birshreshtha Munshi Abdur Rouf Public College (BMARPC), located at the prestigious Peelkhana campus in Dhaka, is dedicated to shaping future leaders, officers, and responsible citizens.

Our cadets receive comprehensive training under military supervision from Bangladesh Air Force personnel and Cadet Under Officers. Training modules encompass aviation theory, aeromodelling, drill and ceremonial parades, weapon handling, map reading, navigation, meteorology, first aid, and community disaster relief operations.
